## TRAINING

## COURSES

You need a highly-qualified service in order to use your SANY products safely and effectively. This is why SANY is convinced that outstanding training for our service staff is the key to success, and why we understand the value of training for our dealer network, so that we can offer you excellent service. The “SANY Centre of Excellence” programme guarantees comprehensive training for our service staff, which then helps to reduce operating and maintenance costs and maximises profitability.

We can also offer you on-site training courses for all SANY products in conjunction with our partners. By doing this, we ensure that the driver/operator is completely familiar with the relevant system, and is capable of operating the machinery perfectly and exploiting its potential fully. This comprehensive training course serves as an introduction to new machines, as a course for new staff members or for refreshing the knowledge of experienced operators.
