# SANY SYL956H5 — Diesel Wheel Loader (dry or wet brake)

The SANY SYL956H5 is the diesel wheel loader in ThinkQuip's SANY range. It is
compared against the SANY SW956E electric loader in the ThinkQuip savings
calculator. It comes in two brake configurations — **dry brake** and **wet
brake** — which differ **only in selling price**; the specifications,
consumption and running costs are identical. ThinkQuip is an authorized SANY
distributor in South Africa.

## Price

- SANY SYL956H5 diesel, **dry brake**: R1,850,000 excluding VAT
- SANY SYL956H5 diesel, **wet brake**: R2,200,000 excluding VAT

The wet-brake variant costs R350,000 more upfront than the dry-brake variant.
VAT in South Africa is 15%; the calculator can show figures VAT-inclusive or
VAT-exclusive.

## Key specifications (SANY SYL956H5)

- Engine: Cummins QSL8.9-C220 III, ~164 kW @ 2200 rpm
- Fuel tank: 300 L
- Operating weight: 17,100 kg
- Rated payload: 5,000 kg
- Bucket capacity: 3.0 m³
- Tyres: L5
- Rated consumption: 14 L/h (typical, spec-sheet figure)

## Warranty (SANY SYL956H5)

- Complete machine: 24 months / 4,000 h

## Running costs in the calculator (SANY SYL956H5)

In the ThinkQuip TCO calculator the SYL956H5's running cost has two parts:

1. **Diesel fuel** — consumption ranges from 10 L/h in light operation up to
   16 L/h in the heaviest operation, multiplied by the customer's diesel price
   (the editable default placeholder is R23.50/L — always confirm the real
   rate). A fuel-theft uplift is applied depending on how well the site is
   controlled: about 10% for a low-control environment, 3.5% for a moderately
   controlled site, or 1% for a well-monitored site. Diesel price is escalated
   at +6% per year in projections.
2. **Routine engine service** — a continuous R29 per operating hour (R29,000
   per 1,000 hours), escalated at +6% per year. There is no separate engine
   overhaul event in the model.

SANY's scheduled-maintenance figures for the SYL956H5 (spec-sheet data,
separate from the calculator's running-cost model): R74,873 per year at
2,000 h/year utilization, or R91,373 per year at 3,000 h/year.
