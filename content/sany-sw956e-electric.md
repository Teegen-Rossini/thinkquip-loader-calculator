# SANY SW956E — Electric Wheel Loader

The SANY SW956E is the electric wheel loader in ThinkQuip's SANY range. It is
compared against the SANY SYL956H5 diesel loader in the ThinkQuip savings
calculator. ThinkQuip is an authorized SANY distributor in South Africa.

## Price

The SANY SW956E electric loader has a **list price of R3,450,000 excluding
VAT**. The 320 kW charger is **included** in that price — there is no separate
charging-infrastructure cost. In the calculator the price is an editable
input defaulting to the list price; when the salesperson enters a lower
price, a discount-off-list summary is shown and every figure follows the
entered price. VAT in South Africa is 15%; the calculator can show figures
VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive.

## Key specifications (SANY SW956E)

- Operating weight: 20,000 kg
- Rated payload: 5,800 kg
- Bucket capacity: 3.5 m³
- Tyres: L5
- Rated consumption: 38 kWh/h (typical heavy duty, spec-sheet figure)
- Operating life: 30,000 – 35,000 h

## Battery and charging (SANY SW956E)

- Battery capacity: 422 kWh
- Charger: 320 kW, 2 charging guns (included in the purchase price)
- Charge time 20% → 80%: 0.8 hours
- Charge time 20% → 100%: 1.5 hours
- Work hours per charge: 7 – 9 hours
- Battery cycle life: 4,000+ cycles

## Warranty (SANY SW956E)

- Complete machine: 24 months / 5,000 h
- Battery, drive motor & electric control: 60 months / 10,000 h
- Axle, hydraulic pump & gearbox: 24 months / 5,000 h

## Running costs in the calculator (SANY SW956E)

In the ThinkQuip TCO calculator the SW956E's running cost is electricity only:
consumption ranges from 20 kWh/h in light operation up to 40 kWh/h in the
heaviest operation, multiplied by the customer's electricity price (the
editable default placeholder is R2.80/kWh — always confirm the customer's real
tariff). The electric machine carries **no mechanical-service cost line** in
the model — a deliberate long-term advantage over diesel. Electricity price is
escalated at +8% per year in projections.

SANY's scheduled-maintenance figures for the SW956E (spec-sheet data, separate
from the calculator's running-cost model): R53,877 per year at 2,000 h/year
utilization, or R75,377 per year at 3,000 h/year.

## Battery replacement (SANY SW956E — background knowledge, not shown in the tool)

The battery reaches replacement at **30,000 operating hours** — beyond the
calculator's 15,000-hour chart and beyond the first owner's typical machine
lifecycle. The base replacement cost is R1,120,000, and battery costs are
projected to **decline about 5% per year**, so the cost at the replacement
year is expected to be lower than today's figure. This is not displayed
anywhere on screen or in the printed brochure; bring it up only if the
customer asks about the battery's life or replacement.
