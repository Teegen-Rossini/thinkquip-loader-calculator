# SANY loader models, prices and specifications (ThinkQuip savings calculator)

The ThinkQuip savings calculator compares the following SANY machines. All prices are in South African Rand, excluding VAT (15% VAT can be toggled on in the tool). The two SYL956H5 diesel brake variants (dry vs wet) have identical specifications — only the selling price differs.

## SANY SW956E (Electric)

- Machine type: Wheel Loader
- Energy type: electric
- Selling price: R3 450 000 excluding VAT (R3 967 500 including 15% VAT) — the 320 kW charger is included in this price; there is no separate charging-infrastructure cost
- Operating weight: 20 000 kg
- Rated payload: 5 800 kg
- Bucket capacity: 3.5 m³
- Tyres: L5
- Rated consumption (spec sheet, typical heavy duty): 38 kWh/h
- Battery capacity: 422 kWh
- Charger: 320 kW, 2 charge guns (included in the purchase price)
- Charge time: 0.8 h (20% → 80%); 1.5 h (20% → 100%)
- Work per charge: 7 – 9 h
- Battery cycle life: 4,000+ cycles
- Operating life: 30,000 – 35,000 h
- Warranty: 5,000 h / 2 years
- Warranty tiers:
  - Complete machine: 24 months / 5,000 h
  - Battery, drive motor & electric control: 60 months / 10,000 h
  - Axle, hydraulic pump & gearbox: 24 months / 5,000 h
- Scheduled maintenance (SANY spec-sheet figures): R53 877/year at 2,000 h/year utilization; R75 377/year at 3,000 h/year. (These are printed spec-sheet figures — the cost model instead uses R0/h for electric.)
- Battery replacement: R1 120 000 (today's cost) at 30 000 h — beyond the 15,000 h comparison chart and beyond the first owner's typical lifecycle, so it is not part of the TCO comparison and is not shown anywhere in the tool or brochure. Battery prices are projected to DECLINE 5% per year, so the actual future cost is lower.
